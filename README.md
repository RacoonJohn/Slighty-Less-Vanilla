## 🧩 Modules
 
Each feature is separated into its own datapack, so you can pick and choose exactly what you want in your game.
 
## ⚙️ Config
 
All SLV datapacks can be configured through a single command:
 
```
/function slv:api/config
```
 
## 🪂 Elytra
 
The weight of your armor now affects how you fly.
 
Heavier armor means less air drag but a faster fall — lighter armor means more drag but a gentler glide.
 
> **Example:** Netherite armor gives you less air drag and a faster fall speed, so you fly faster overall — but you'll burn through fireworks more quickly.
 
## 🔧 Mending
 
A complete rework of the vanilla **Mending** enchantment (with *Minecraft's experimental features*).
 
Instead of repairing gear with XP, equipment that reaches 0 durability no longer breaks — it becomes unusable, just like an elytra.

Recommended to use [Librarian's Balance](https://modrinth.com/datapack/librarians-balance)
## 🧈 Netherite Alloy
 
Craft netherite armor with a **gold or iron base** instead of the usual diamond gear, changing its durability, enchantability, and other stats along the way.
